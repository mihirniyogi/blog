## What's RSS?

RSS is a simple, long-standing way to follow websites. A **feed reader** app checks the sites you follow and shows new posts in one place. There are no accounts to create, no algorithm and no tracking.

## How to subscribe

1. Install a feed reader. Popular ones include NetNewsWire (Mac and iPhone), Feedly and Inoreader, and Thunderbird can read feeds too.
2. Copy the feed address above.
3. Add it in your reader, usually under "Add feed" or "Subscribe".

New posts will then show up in your reader as soon as they're published.
